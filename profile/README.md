## Operating Principles

- **Evidence first**: every published claim links to sealed primary-source records.
- **Deterministic gates**: automated triage flags leads; human editorial review and sign-off are required before publication.
- **Verifiable custody**: append-only audit logs and offline-verifiable provenance chains.
